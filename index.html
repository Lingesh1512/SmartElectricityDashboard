Energy Tips

❄️

Reduce unnecessary AC usage

Set it to 24–26 °C and use a fan alongside to cool rooms faster.

💡

Switch off unused lights

Turn lights off when you leave a room and use LED bulbs.

🌟

Use energy-efficient appliances

Look for high star-rated appliances; they pay back in lower bills.

🔌

Avoid standby power consumption

Unplug chargers and TVs, or use a switched power strip.

☀️

Use natural lighting

Open curtains in the daytime instead of switching lights on.
